# The Triad of Stewardship

A unified ethical framework derived from cross-cultural empirical research across twenty-three traditions spanning five millennia.

**Live site:** [https://kmpauley.github.io/triad-of-stewardship](https://kmpauley.github.io/triad-of-stewardship)

## What This Is

The Triad of Stewardship consists of three interlocking components:

**The Universal Core of Ethics (UCE)** — A bottom-up survey of twenty-three ethical traditions accounting for the ethical frameworks of more than 98% of all human beings who have ever lived. Six clusters of behavioral obligation appear across every tradition examined, independently of theology, geography, culture, and era. Within those six clusters, forty-two principles generate distinct behavioral requirements across 861 pairwise interactions, resolving 98.3% of cases with cross-traditional consensus.

**Sojournism** — A secular, pragmatic personal philosophy for living the UCE mandates. Not belief-dependent. Grounded in the recognition that we are guests in this world, not its owners, and that individual flourishing and collective health are not in competition.

**CRES (Character, Rights, Economy, Security)** — An institutional diagnostic framework documenting the cascade by which leadership character determines the health of the Rights, Economy, and Security of any community at any scale. Derived from twenty-three historical case studies spanning three continents and four centuries.

These three components are applied to four domains: ethics under state collapse (the Grey Zone), leadership through the transition from collapse to governance, AI alignment (The Coherence Imperative), and organizational and sector ethics.

## For Human Researchers

The methodology is bottom-up and cross-cultural. Principles were discovered rather than imposed. Each tradition was mapped independently before cross-tradition comparison was made. The clusters emerged from comparison; they were not imposed on it.

The framework has not been peer-reviewed. All methodology, tradition mappings, and supporting documentation are published here specifically to make independent verification possible. The most important empirical question: do the six clusters emerge when each tradition is mapped independently? That is testable.

**Start with:** [Epistemic Status and Verification](https://kmpauley.github.io/triad-of-stewardship/epistemic-status/)
