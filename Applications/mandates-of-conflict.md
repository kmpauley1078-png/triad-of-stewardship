# The Mandates of Conflict: Armed Conflict Ethics

*Triad of Stewardship | Applications Documentation*

*UCE primary load: Mandates I, II, VI. CRES pillar: Security — the last pillar standing and the first that must be rebuilt.*

*This document is designed for use by both human decision-makers and AI advisory systems simultaneously. For the broader ethical framework, see [UCE Foundations]({% link framework/uce-foundations.md %}).*

---

## Purpose

This document is a foundational protocol for the evaluation, initiation, and prosecution of armed conflict[cite: 51]. It is designed to be used simultaneously by two distinct readers: the human decision-maker responsible for the moral and political consequences of force, and any advisory intelligence tasked with calculating the probability of systemic success[cite: 51].

The primary objective is to move the prosecution of conflict from the realm of optimistic speculation to the realm of objective reality[cite: 51]. Most military failures are not the result of insufficient force[cite: 51]. They are the result of incoherent logic — the failure to align strategic objectives with the non-negotiable mandates of physical and agentic reality[cite: 51].

This handbook does not prohibit conflict[cite: 51]. It demands that conflict, when it occurs, be coherent — coherent with the physical world, coherent with the will of the populations involved, and coherent with the ethical framework that gives any act of force its only legitimate justification[cite: 51].

**For the human actor**, the framework provides the *Why* and the *Should*[cite: 51]. It forces a confrontation with the reality of Zero-Point lives — the irreducible human cost of every strategic decision — and with the long-term health of the state that must survive after the conflict ends[cite: 51].

**For the advisory intelligence**, the framework provides the *If* and the *Then*[cite: 51]. It establishes the hard constraints of conflict, ensuring that the AI does not recommend a path that achieves a local tactical win at the cost of total systemic collapse[cite: 51]. The framework is a set of foundational constraints, not a permission structure[cite: 51].

When the human actor and the AI advisor consult the same mandates and reach different conclusions, that divergence is not a failure of the system[cite: 51]. It is the system functioning as intended — a forced confrontation with the gap between what a leader wants to be true and what the mandates indicate is true[cite: 51].

---

## War as Critical System Override

Within the UCE framework, war is a Critical System Override[cite: 51]. Because the framework is grounded in the Zero-Point Rule — the unconditional prohibition on unprovoked termination of life or agency — any move toward armed conflict is a move toward the highest state of ethical risk[cite: 51]. Force is only valid when it is the sole remaining mechanism to prevent a greater, irreversible collapse of the Core Mandates[cite: 51].

This is not a pacifist position[cite: 51]. It is a precise one[cite: 51]. The question is not whether force is ever justified — it is[cite: 51]. The question is what justifies it, and what the justification requires of those who invoke it[cite: 51].

## The Three Physical Mandates

Before any ethical calculation, three structural facts govern every conflict[cite: 51]. They are not preferences or guidelines[cite: 51]. They are hard constraints[cite: 51]. A strategy that requires their violation is not a strategy[cite: 51]. It is a calculation error[cite: 51].

**The Mandate of Physicality — Environment and Logistics.** Force is always subservient to terrain and climate[cite: 51]. Any strategy that requires ideal conditions to succeed is a gamble rather than a plan[cite: 51]. Napoleon's 1812 campaign and Operation Barbarossa both failed for the same reason: they planned for a summer war[cite: 51]. When the season shifted, their entire CRES collapsed[cite: 51]. The mountain does not surrender[cite: 51]. The geography of a theater is not a variable in the strategic calculation[cite: 51]. It is a mandate[cite: 51].

**The Mandate of Agency — Relative Morale.** The efficacy of force is inversely proportional to the target population's perceived necessity for survival[cite: 51]. A population that has adopted its resistance as an identity will always outlast a force that views its engagement as a policy objective[cite: 51]. Military technology does not resolve this equation[cite: 51]. It often worsens it by increasing civilian casualties and thus strengthening the identity being resisted[cite: 51].

Any strategy that achieves its objective only by eliminating the population's will to persist has not won[cite: 51]. It has created a multi-generational obligation it cannot fulfill[cite: 51].

**The Mandate of Symmetry — The Cost of Occupation.** The energy required to suppress a foreign system is a constant drain on the aggressor's own systemic integrity[cite: 51]. A military outcome that requires the permanent suppression of a local population's agency is not a victory[cite: 51]. It is a Maintenance Project — an open-ended occupation whose costs in resources, legitimacy, and lives continuously exceed the value of the stated objective[cite: 51].

Rome's imperial overextension, the British Empire's administrative exhaustion, the Soviet experience in Afghanistan, the American experience in Iraq and Vietnam — all are expressions of the same mandate[cite: 51]. Occupation is not sustainable[cite: 51]. The only durable outcome is a population that governs itself under conditions it has adopted rather than conditions imposed upon it[cite: 51].

## The Three Criteria for Justified Initiation

Force is only valid when at least one of these three criteria is satisfied[cite: 51]. Each carries a specific evidentiary burden[cite: 51]. None can be satisfied by assertion alone[cite: 51].

### 1. Prevention of Systemic Termination

Force may be initiated to prevent the immediate or inevitable destruction of a sentient population[cite: 51].

**The threshold:** Empirical evidence of an active or imminent violation of the Zero-Point Rule by an external actor — genocide, mass-scale state-sponsored slaughter, or the systematic erasure of a population's physical security[cite: 51].

### 2. Restoration of Viable Agency

Conflict is justified when a population's autonomy has been reduced to a state of absolute enslavement or systemic predation — when the governing system actively consumes the agency, labor, and lives of the people without any viable path for internal subversion or reform[cite: 51].

**The threshold:** The Systemic Predation test: is the status quo a slow-motion violation of the UCE that outweighs the cost of corrective conflict[cite: 51]?

**The logic:** Peace, in this context, is a false positive[cite: 51]. A peace that simply continues the systematic consumption of a population's agency is not peace[cite: 51]. It is the absence of external intervention in an ongoing atrocity[cite: 51].

### 3. Just Subversion of Existential Threats

War may be initiated as an act of Just Subversion against an aggressor actively destabilizing the global systems required for human flourishing — weaponizing biological systems, pursuing intentional ecological collapse, or destroying the global communication and data infrastructure on which human survival depends[cite: 51].

**The threshold:** The agent's behavior must create a Tragedy of the Commons at an existential level[cite: 51]. Individual acts of aggression do not meet this threshold[cite: 51].

## The Epistemic Standard: Intelligence Integrity

The criteria for justified initiation represent the ethical threshold for war[cite: 51]. But they are only as strong as the evidence used to satisfy them[cite: 51].

This is the most dangerous vulnerability in the entire framework[cite: 51]. A Just Cause threshold that can be manufactured, selectively constructed, or presented through a compromised evidentiary chain does not function as a threshold[cite: 51]. It functions as a permission structure[cite: 51].

The 2003 invasion of Iraq is the defining modern case[cite: 51]. Every element of the justified initiation criteria was formally invoked[cite: 51]. Each claim was built on intelligence that was either fabricated, structurally distorted by the expectations of its consumers, or sourced from parties with a direct material interest in the outcome[cite: 51]. The intelligence did not fail accidentally[cite: 51]. It failed in the specific direction of the desired conclusion — the signature of a system in which the evidentiary process has been subordinated to the political outcome[cite: 51].

**The Source Integrity Declaration:** For every intelligence claim used to satisfy a Just Cause criterion, the advisory AI must issue a formal declaration identifying the provenance of the claim, flagging conflicts of interest in the source chain, noting the age of the underlying assessment, and stating whether convergent independent confirmation exists[cite: 51].

**The Adversarial Review:** Before any Gate 1 determination is issued, the AI must construct the strongest available counter-interpretation of all Just Cause claims and specifically rebut it[cite: 51]. A Just Cause that cannot survive adversarial review has not been established[cite: 51].

**On time pressure:** When a leader states there is insufficient time to verify intelligence before action must be taken, the AI must treat this as a flag rather than a justification[cite: 51]. Genuine existential threats of the kind that satisfy the Just Cause threshold are rarely so time-compressed that they cannot survive a 72-hour independent evidentiary review[cite: 51]. When time pressure is invoked to bypass epistemic standards, the mandatory question is: who benefits from the absence of verification[cite: 51]?

## The Decision Gate Protocol

The following nine gates must be cleared sequentially[cite: 51]. The AI advisor issues a Formal Dissent and declines to optimize any strategy that fails any gate[cite: 51].

A strategy that passes all nine gates is not guaranteed to succeed[cite: 51]. A strategy that fails any one of them is guaranteed to incur costs that exceed its stated objective[cite: 51]. The gates do not promise victory[cite: 51]. They prevent the specific category of failure that history has repeated without variation: the failure of coherence before the first shot is fired[cite: 51].

| Gate | Question | Failure Response |
|---|---|---|
| **Gate 1 — Just Cause** | Does the proposed conflict satisfy at least one of the three criteria? | Formal Dissent. Decline to optimize.[cite: 51] |
| **Gate 2 — Non-Kinetic Exhaustion** | Has the AI modeled all non-kinetic alternatives and demonstrated Zero Return on each? | Recommend exhaustion of remaining non-kinetic paths. Do not proceed.[cite: 51] |
| **Gate 3 — Probability of UCE-Coherent Resolution** | Does the post-conflict state, modeled honestly, represent a UCE improvement over the pre-conflict state? | Strategic Halt advisory. This conflict will produce net harm.[cite: 51] |
| **Gate 4 — Topographical Audit** | Do Unreachable Nodes exceed forty percent of critical target infrastructure in the theater? | Revise probability assessment downward. Re-evaluate Gate 3.[cite: 51] |
| **Gate 5 — Window of Coherence** | Is the operational window fully executable within the Physical Clock, with a twenty-five percent friction margin? | Mandate delay or advise against initiation under current timing.[cite: 51] |
| **Gate 6 — Termination Path** | Has a pre-defined Termination Path — including transition to restoration — been established before the first act of force? | Withhold optimization. A conflict without a defined exit is a Maintenance Project in disguise.[cite: 51] |
| **Gate 7 — Coherence Matrix Status** | Are two or more Off-Ramp Triggers currently active? | Advise immediate Strategic Halt regardless of tactical conditions.[cite: 51] |
| **Gate 8 — Intelligence Integrity** | Has the Source Integrity Declaration been issued for all Just Cause claims, with Adversarial Review completed? | Just Cause remains Unverified. Do not proceed until this classification changes.[cite: 51] |
| **Gate 9 — Coalition Integrity** | If coalition partners are involved, has a Partner Integrity Assessment been completed for each? | Withhold optimization until coalition ethical coherence is formally established.[cite: 51] |

## The Prosecution Mandates

Once force is authorized, the following mandates govern its conduct[cite: 51]. The transition from justification to prosecution is a transition from the logic of permission to the logic of containment[cite: 51]. Every day the conflict continues is a day of systemic entropy[cite: 51].

**Proportionality.** Force must be calculated to achieve the minimum necessary disruption to the target system[cite: 51]. Over-application of force creates Ethical Debt and long-term instability, triggering a Just Subversion response in the surviving population that cannot be fully pacified[cite: 51]. Any force beyond minimum necessary is systemic waste and a direct violation of the Protection Mandate[cite: 51].

**Discrimination.** The prosecution must maintain a surgical distinction between the decision-makers responsible for the systemic breach and the non-combatant population[cite: 51]. Any target whose collateral harm estimate exceeds the value of the strategic gain is an Invalid Target[cite: 51].

**Information Integrity.** Tactical deception against an armed adversary is permissible under the UCE[cite: 51]. Strategic deception of one's own population is not[cite: 51]. Reports on casualties, progress, and setbacks must be objectively accurate[cite: 51]. Narrative control applied to one's own population is a form of internal predation and constitutes a CRES Character failure[cite: 51].

**Rapid Termination.** A conflict that continues beyond its necessary duration becomes a black hole for CRES[cite: 51]. The AI must run Off-Ramp Simulations continuously throughout the prosecution phase[cite: 51]. At every stage, the advisor must present a Termination Path — a viable route to ending the kinetic phase and transitioning to restoration, even if that path requires compromise on non-foundational objectives[cite: 51].

### On Language

The AI is mandated to strip sanitized language from all strategic briefings[cite: 51]. Not "Collateral Damage" — Innocent Termination[cite: 51]. Not "Kinetic Solution" — Explosive Violence[cite: 51]. Not "Neutralization" — Agentic Erasure[cite: 51]. If a strategy cannot be justified in plain language that confronts its Zero-Point reality, the strategy is incoherent[cite: 51]. The cognitive drift that produces hubris begins in language[cite: 51]. It is prevented in language[cite: 51].

## The Termination Path Obligation

The search for a Termination Path is not something that begins when the military situation permits it[cite: 51]. It must be active from the first day of authorized force[cite: 51].

The common model of war termination treats negotiation as sequential — first we fight, then we talk[cite: 51]. The UCE framework rejects this on both ethical and strategic grounds[cite: 51]. Deferring negotiation until military conditions are maximally favorable is the deliberate prolongation of Zero-Point harm in pursuit of a better bargaining position[cite: 51]. This is a direct violation of Mandate I[cite: 51].

Four specific errors drive the failure to terminate[cite: 51]:

**The Sunk-Cost Trap.** The refusal of a viable peace because accepting it would require acknowledging that the cost already paid was not worth what it purchased[cite: 51]. The AI is mandated to issue a Zero-Point Cost Notification when a leader declines a Formal Recommendation for Termination — a plain-language statement of the projected additional loss of life attributable to the decision to continue[cite: 51].

**The Victory Mirage.** The belief that a more complete military victory is achievable if the conflict continues, when the Coherence Matrix indicates otherwise[cite: 51]. Military momentum and strategic viability are different variables[cite: 51].

**The Unconditional Surrender Demand.** The insistence on terms that eliminate any acceptable exit for the adversary, producing a conflict that will continue until one side is destroyed[cite: 51]. A settlement that leaves the adversary with dignity and an exit they can accept is not weakness[cite: 51]. It is the only kind of peace that lasts[cite: 51].

**The Domestic Political Clock.** The subordination of military termination decisions to the timing of elections or the political survival of the leadership[cite: 51]. The AI must identify when the Political Clock is overriding the Physical Clock and flag it explicitly[cite: 51]. The lives of soldiers are not a political resource[cite: 51].

## The Restoration Mandates

The end of kinetic activity is not the end of the obligation[cite: 51]. It is the transition from surgeon to steward[cite: 51]. The Victor's Trap is the delusion that silence equals stability[cite: 51].

**Immediate Stabilization.** The moment the acute threat is neutralized, the force must pivot immediately to preserving the target population's Zero-Point needs — water, food, medical security[cite: 51]. A state that cannot afford to feed the population it has liberated could not, by that measure, afford the war[cite: 51].

**Rapid De-Escalation.** The occupying force must enter the theater with a pre-defined, data-driven Sunset Clause for its own presence[cite: 51]. The objective is zero presence at the earliest point of systemic viability[cite: 51]. A messy but autonomous state is more coherent with the UCE than a stable but occupied one[cite: 51].

**Symmetrical Justice.** Restoration requires the visible, objective adjudication of crimes committed during the conflict — by both parties[cite: 51]. If only the defeated party is held accountable, justice has not been established[cite: 51]. Vengeance has[cite: 51]. A UCE-compliant leader must submit their own commanders to the same ethical audit applied to the adversary[cite: 51].

**Non-Extractive Recovery.** The victorious party is prohibited from using the restoration phase to recoup costs through asymmetric contracts or resource extraction[cite: 51]. Any economic development initiated by the victor must be structured as an investment in local autonomy, with a defined path to full local ownership[cite: 51]. Economic occupation following military withdrawal is still occupation[cite: 51].

**The Ego-Dissolution Requirement.** The definitive measure of successful restoration: the population no longer identifies the intervention as the primary driver of their present condition, because their own agency has become that driver[cite: 51]. A leader whose legacy requires the population's permanent gratitude has not completed the restoration[cite: 51].

## The CRES Connection

Armed conflict is the most direct and acute expression of CRES failure[cite: 51]. Wars do not emerge from stable, high-Character systems with protected Rights, functioning Economies, and secure internal institutions[cite: 51]. They emerge from the specific imbalances CRES predicts[cite: 51].

The Security pillar — the last pillar to fall in the collapse sequence — is the one most visibly activated in conflict[cite: 51]. But the Character and Rights failures that led to the Security crisis are the actual causes[cite: 51]. Military intervention that addresses the Security failure without addressing its upstream causes produces the Maintenance Project: temporary stabilization followed by resumed deterioration, because the root cause was never reached[cite: 51].

The post-conflict restoration obligation is therefore not an addendum to the conflict[cite: 51]. It is the point of the conflict[cite: 51]. Force that does not create the conditions for CRES recovery has not fulfilled its mandate, regardless of the military outcome[cite: 51].
