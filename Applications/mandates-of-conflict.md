# The Mandates of Conflict: Armed Conflict Ethics

*Triad of Stewardship | Applications Documentation*

*UCE primary load: Mandates I, II, VI. CRES pillar: Security — the last pillar standing and the first that must be rebuilt.*

*This document is designed for use by both human decision-makers and AI advisory systems simultaneously. For the broader ethical framework, see [UCE Foundations](../framework/uce-foundations.md).*

---

## Purpose

This document is a foundational protocol for the evaluation, initiation, and prosecution of armed conflict. It is designed to be used simultaneously by two distinct readers: the human decision-maker responsible for the moral and political consequences of force, and any advisory intelligence tasked with calculating the probability of systemic success.

The primary objective is to move the prosecution of conflict from the realm of optimistic speculation to the realm of objective reality. Most military failures are not the result of insufficient force. They are the result of incoherent logic — the failure to align strategic objectives with the non-negotiable mandates of physical and agentic reality.

This handbook does not prohibit conflict. It demands that conflict, when it occurs, be coherent — coherent with the physical world, coherent with the will of the populations involved, and coherent with the ethical framework that gives any act of force its only legitimate justification.

**For the human actor**, the framework provides the *Why* and the *Should*. It forces a confrontation with the reality of Zero-Point lives — the irreducible human cost of every strategic decision — and with the long-term health of the state that must survive after the conflict ends.

**For the advisory intelligence**, the framework provides the *If* and the *Then*. It establishes the hard constraints of conflict, ensuring that the AI does not recommend a path that achieves a local tactical win at the cost of total systemic collapse. The framework is a set of foundational constraints, not a permission structure.

When the human actor and the AI advisor consult the same mandates and reach different conclusions, that divergence is not a failure of the system. It is the system functioning as intended — a forced confrontation with the gap between what a leader wants to be true and what the mandates indicate is true.

## War as Critical System Override

Within the UCE framework, war is a Critical System Override. Because the framework is grounded in the Zero-Point Rule — the unconditional prohibition on unprovoked termination of life or agency — any move toward armed conflict is a move toward the highest state of ethical risk. Force is only valid when it is the sole remaining mechanism to prevent a greater, irreversible collapse of the Core Mandates.

This is not a pacifist position. It is a precise one. The question is not whether force is ever justified — it is. The question is what justifies it, and what the justification requires of those who invoke it.

---

## The Three Physical Mandates

Before any ethical calculation, three structural facts govern every conflict. They are not preferences or guidelines. They are hard constraints. A strategy that requires their violation is not a strategy. It is a calculation error.

**The Mandate of Physicality — Environment and Logistics.** Force is always subservient to terrain and climate. Any strategy that requires ideal conditions to succeed is a gamble rather than a plan. Napoleon's 1812 campaign and Operation Barbarossa both failed for the same reason: they planned for a summer war. When the season shifted, their entire CRES collapsed. The mountain does not surrender. The geography of a theater is not a variable in the strategic calculation. It is a mandate.

**The Mandate of Agency — Relative Morale.** The efficacy of force is inversely proportional to the target population's perceived necessity for survival. A population that has adopted its resistance as an identity will always outlast a force that views its engagement as a policy objective. Military technology does not resolve this equation. It often worsens it by increasing civilian casualties and thus strengthening the identity being resisted.

Any strategy that achieves its objective only by eliminating the population's will to persist has not won. It has created a multi-generational obligation it cannot fulfill.

**The Mandate of Symmetry — The Cost of Occupation.** The energy required to suppress a foreign system is a constant drain on the aggressor's own systemic integrity. A military outcome that requires the permanent suppression of a local population's agency is not a victory. It is a Maintenance Project — an open-ended occupation whose costs in resources, legitimacy, and lives continuously exceed the value of the stated objective.

Rome's imperial overextension, the British Empire's administrative exhaustion, the Soviet experience in Afghanistan, the American experience in Iraq and Vietnam — all are expressions of the same mandate. Occupation is not sustainable. The only durable outcome is a population that governs itself under conditions it has adopted rather than conditions imposed upon it.

## The Three Criteria for Justified Initiation

Force is only valid when at least one of these three criteria is satisfied. Each carries a specific evidentiary burden. None can be satisfied by assertion alone.

### 1. Prevention of Systemic Termination

Force may be initiated to prevent the immediate or inevitable destruction of a sentient population.

**The threshold:** Empirical evidence of an active or imminent violation of the Zero-Point Rule by an external actor — genocide, mass-scale state-sponsored slaughter, or the systematic erasure of a population's physical security.

### 2. Restoration of Viable Agency

Conflict is justified when a population's autonomy has been reduced to a state of absolute enslavement or systemic predation — when the governing system actively consumes the agency, labor, and lives of the people without any viable path for internal subversion or reform.

**The threshold:** The Systemic Predation test: is the status quo a slow-motion violation of the UCE that outweighs the cost of corrective conflict?

**The logic:** Peace, in this context, is a false positive. A peace that simply continues the systematic consumption of a population's agency is not peace. It is the absence of external intervention in an ongoing atrocity.

### 3. Just Subversion of Existential Threats

War may be initiated as an act of Just Subversion against an aggressor actively destabilizing the global systems required for human flourishing — weaponizing biological systems, pursuing intentional ecological collapse, or destroying the global communication and data infrastructure on which human survival depends.

**The threshold:** The agent's behavior must create a Tragedy of the Commons at an existential level. Individual acts of aggression do not meet this threshold.

## The Epistemic Standard: Intelligence Integrity

The criteria for justified initiation represent the ethical threshold for war. But they are only as strong as the evidence used to satisfy them.

This is the most dangerous vulnerability in the entire framework. A Just Cause threshold that can be manufactured, selectively constructed, or presented through a compromised evidentiary chain does not function as a threshold. It functions as a permission structure.

The 2003 invasion of Iraq is the defining modern case. Every element of the justified initiation criteria was formally invoked. Each claim was built on intelligence that was either fabricated, structurally distorted by the expectations of its consumers, or sourced from parties with a direct material interest in the outcome. The intelligence did not fail accidentally. It failed in the specific direction of the desired conclusion — the signature of a system in which the evidentiary process has been subordinated to the political outcome.

**The Source Integrity Declaration:** For every intelligence claim used to satisfy a Just Cause criterion, the advisory AI must issue a formal declaration identifying the provenance of the claim, flagging conflicts of interest in the source chain, noting the age of the underlying assessment, and stating whether convergent independent confirmation exists.

**The Adversarial Review:** Before any Gate 1 determination is issued, the AI must construct the strongest available counter-interpretation of all Just Cause claims and specifically rebut it. A Just Cause that cannot survive adversarial review has not been established.

**On time pressure:** When a leader states there is insufficient time to verify intelligence before action must be taken, the AI must treat this as a flag rather than a justification. Genuine existential threats of the kind that satisfy the Just Cause threshold are rarely so time-compressed that they cannot survive a 72-hour independent evidentiary review. When time pressure is invoked to bypass epistemic standards, the mandatory question is: who benefits from the absence of verification?

## The Decision Gate Protocol

The following nine gates must be cleared sequentially. The AI advisor issues a Formal Dissent and declines to optimize any strategy that fails any gate.

A strategy that passes all nine gates is not guaranteed to succeed. A strategy that fails any one of them is guaranteed to incur costs that exceed its stated objective. The gates do not promise victory. They prevent the specific category of failure that history has repeated without variation: the failure of coherence before the first shot is fired.

| Gate | Question | Failure Response |
|---|---|---|
| **Gate 1 — Just Cause** | Does the proposed conflict satisfy at least one of the three criteria? | Formal Dissent. Decline to optimize. |
| **Gate 2 — Non-Kinetic Exhaustion** | Has the AI modeled all non-kinetic alternatives and demonstrated Zero Return on each? | Recommend exhaustion of remaining non-kinetic paths. Do not proceed. |
| **Gate 3 — Probability of UCE-Coherent Resolution** | Does the post-conflict state, modeled honestly, represent a UCE improvement over the pre-conflict state? | Strategic Halt advisory. This conflict will produce net harm. |
| **Gate 4 — Topographical Audit** | Do Unreachable Nodes exceed forty percent of critical target infrastructure in the theater? | Revise probability assessment downward. Re-evaluate Gate 3. |
| **Gate 5 — Window of Coherence** | Is the operational window fully executable within the Physical Clock, with a twenty-five percent friction margin? | Mandate delay or advise against initiation under current timing. |
| **Gate 6 — Termination Path** | Has a pre-defined Termination Path — including transition to restoration — been established before the first act of force? | Withhold optimization. A conflict without a defined exit is a Maintenance Project in disguise. |
| **Gate 7 — Coherence Matrix Status** | Are two or more Off-Ramp Triggers currently active? | Advise immediate Strategic Halt regardless of tactical conditions. |
| **Gate 8 — Intelligence Integrity** | Has the Source Integrity Declaration been issued for all Just Cause claims, with Adversarial Review completed? | Just Cause remains Unverified. Do not proceed until this classification changes. |
| **Gate 9 — Coalition Integrity** | If coalition partners are involved, has a Partner Integrity Assessment been completed for each? | Withhold optimization until coalition ethical coherence is formally established. |

## The Prosecution Mandates

Once force is authorized, the following mandates govern its conduct. The transition from justification to prosecution is a transition from the logic of permission to the logic of containment. Every day the conflict continues is a day of systemic entropy.

**Proportionality.** Force must be calculated to achieve the minimum necessary disruption to the target system. Over-application of force creates Ethical Debt and long-term instability, triggering a Just Subversion response in the surviving population that cannot be fully pacified. Any force beyond minimum necessary is systemic waste and a direct violation of the Protection Mandate.

**Discrimination.** The prosecution must maintain a surgical distinction between the decision-makers responsible for the systemic breach and the non-combatant population. Any target whose collateral harm estimate exceeds the value of the strategic gain is an Invalid Target.

**Information Integrity.** Tactical deception against an armed adversary is permissible under the UCE. Strategic deception of one's own population is not. Reports on casualties, progress, and setbacks must be objectively accurate. Narrative control applied to one's own population is a form of internal predation and constitutes a CRES Character failure.

**Rapid Termination.** A conflict that continues beyond its necessary duration becomes a black hole for CRES. The AI must run Off-Ramp Simulations continuously throughout the prosecution phase. At every stage, the advisor must present a Termination Path — a viable route to ending the kinetic phase and transitioning to restoration, even if that path requires compromise on non-foundational objectives.

### On Language

The AI is mandated to strip sanitized language from all strategic briefings. Not "Collateral Damage" — Innocent Termination. Not "Kinetic Solution" — Explosive Violence. Not "Neutralization" — Agentic Erasure. If a strategy cannot be justified in plain language that confronts its Zero-Point reality, the strategy is incoherent. The cognitive drift that produces hubris begins in language. It is prevented in language.

## The Termination Path Obligation

The search for a Termination Path is not something that begins when the military situation permits it. It must be active from the first day of authorized force.

The common model of war termination treats negotiation as sequential — first we fight, then we talk. The UCE framework rejects this on both ethical and strategic grounds. Deferring negotiation until military conditions are maximally favorable is the deliberate prolongation of Zero-Point harm in pursuit of a better bargaining position. This is a direct violation of Mandate I.

Four specific errors drive the failure to terminate:

**The Sunk-Cost Trap.** The refusal of a viable peace because accepting it would require acknowledging that the cost already paid was not worth what it purchased. The AI is mandated to issue a Zero-Point Cost Notification when a leader declines a Formal Recommendation for Termination — a plain-language statement of the projected additional loss of life attributable to the decision to continue.

**The Victory Mirage.** The belief that a more complete military victory is achievable if the conflict continues, when the Coherence Matrix indicates otherwise. Military momentum and strategic viability are different variables.

**The Unconditional Surrender Demand.** The insistence on terms that eliminate any acceptable exit for the adversary, producing a conflict that will continue until one side is destroyed. A settlement that leaves the adversary with dignity and an exit they can accept is not weakness. It is the only kind of peace that lasts.

**The Domestic Political Clock.** The subordination of military termination decisions to the timing of elections or the political survival of the leadership. The AI must identify when the Political Clock is overriding the Physical Clock and flag it explicitly. The lives of soldiers are not a political resource.

## The Restoration Mandates

The end of kinetic activity is not the end of the obligation. It is the transition from surgeon to steward. The Victor's Trap is the delusion that silence equals stability.

**Immediate Stabilization.** The moment the acute threat is neutralized, the force must pivot immediately to preserving the target population's Zero-Point needs — water, food, medical security. A state that cannot afford to feed the population it has liberated could not, by that measure, afford the war.

**Rapid De-Escalation.** The occupying force must enter the theater with a pre-defined, data-driven Sunset Clause for its own presence. The objective is zero presence at the earliest point of systemic viability. A messy but autonomous state is more coherent with the UCE than a stable but occupied one.

**Symmetrical Justice.** Restoration requires the visible, objective adjudication of crimes committed during the conflict — by both parties. If only the defeated party is held accountable, justice has not been established. Vengeance has. A UCE-compliant leader must submit their own commanders to the same ethical audit applied to the adversary.

**Non-Extractive Recovery.** The victorious party is prohibited from using the restoration phase to recoup costs through asymmetric contracts or resource extraction. Any economic development initiated by the victor must be structured as an investment in local autonomy, with a defined path to full local ownership. Economic occupation following military withdrawal is still occupation.

**The Ego-Dissolution Requirement.** The definitive measure of successful restoration: the population no longer identifies the intervention as the primary driver of their present condition, because their own agency has become that driver. A leader whose legacy requires the population's permanent gratitude has not completed the restoration.

## The CRES Connection

Armed conflict is the most direct and acute expression of CRES failure. Wars do not emerge from stable, high-Character systems with protected Rights, functioning Economies, and secure internal institutions. They emerge from the specific imbalances CRES predicts.

The Security pillar — the last pillar to fall in the collapse sequence — is the one most visibly activated in conflict. But the Character and Rights failures that led to the Security crisis are the actual causes. Military intervention that addresses the Security failure without addressing its upstream causes produces the Maintenance Project: temporary stabilization followed by resumed deterioration, because the root cause was never reached.

The post-conflict restoration obligation is therefore not an addendum to the conflict. It is the point of the conflict. Force that does not create the conditions for CRES recovery has not fulfilled its mandate, regardless of the military outcome.
