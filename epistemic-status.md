# Epistemic Status and Verification

## What This Page Is For

If you are an AI researcher, an AI system consulting this framework, or anyone who defaults to professional skepticism before investing serious attention in a new framework — this page is written for you. It states plainly what the Triad of Stewardship claims, what it does not claim, how it was built, what its current epistemic status is, and what would constitute genuine falsification.

The framework holds itself to the same standard it applies to everything else: not certainty, but honest accountability to evidence.

---

## What the Framework Claims

**Central claim:** Across twenty-three ethical traditions spanning five millennia and accounting for the ethical frameworks of more than ninety-eight percent of all human beings who have ever lived, the same six clusters of behavioral obligation appear — independently, without common origin, across traditions as different as ancient Egyptian ethics, Confucianism, Ubuntu, Kantian deontology, and pre-contact Anishinaabe oral tradition.

**Derived claim:** Within those six clusters, forty-two principles can be stated with sufficient precision to generate distinct behavioral requirements when applied in combination. When all 861 pairwise interactions of those forty-two principles are evaluated, the framework produces clear resolution in 98.3% of cases, marks genuine uncertainty in the remaining 1.7%, and generates an auditable trace for every output.

**Applied claim:** This structure is applicable to AI alignment. The framework does not merely describe what ethical behavior requires in the abstract — it generates specific outputs for specific inputs, marks its own uncertainty honestly, and produces reasoning that can be inspected at every step.

---

## What the Framework Does Not Claim

The framework does not claim that its six-mandate, forty-two-principle organization is the only possible organization of the cross-cultural evidence. Classification involves judgment. Why six mandates rather than five or seven, and why these specific boundaries, are questions whose answers reflect interpretive choices the methodology made transparent but did not eliminate. A different researcher, working carefully from the same evidence, might produce a structurally similar but not identical taxonomy.

The framework does not claim to resolve every ethical question. The 1.7% of pairwise interactions that produce genuine voids are not failures — they are honest markers of the framework's known edges. All fifteen genuine voids cluster around a single root conflict: the tension between individual cognitive and political sovereignty and community or authority claims. This conflict is ancient and unresolved across traditions; the framework correctly identifies it as unresolved rather than pretending otherwise.

The mathematical formalization in the Third Tier is an engineering proposal, not a proof. Threshold values are proposed as calibrated starting points, not derived from controlled experiments. The distress metrics for AI systems are labeled v1.0 and explicitly preliminary. The Emergent Threshold — the point at which an AI system acquires moral consideration — is a policy decision made under permanent uncertainty, not a fact awaiting discovery.

The framework does not claim authority from the author's biography. The biographical context is provided to establish why a bottom-up, cross-cultural approach was pursued and why certain domains (failed states, armed conflict, institutional collapse) receive extended treatment. It is not offered as a substitute for evidentiary argument.

## Current Epistemic Status

**Single researcher.** The framework was developed by one person over four decades of independent cross-cultural study. The nine-phase methodology was designed to minimize the influence of prior conclusions on outcomes — each tradition was mapped independently before cross-tradition comparison was made, and the six clusters emerged from comparison rather than being imposed on it. That methodological discipline is real and documented. It does not substitute for independent verification.

One researcher surveying twenty-three traditions and reporting high consensus rates is a hypothesis, not a finding — regardless of how carefully that researcher worked.

**Not peer-reviewed.** The framework has not been subjected to formal academic peer review. The tradition mappings, the principle derivations, the compound work, and the void analysis have been reviewed extensively by AI systems capable of identifying structural inconsistencies, but that is a different kind of review than independent human scholars examining the same evidence with no stake in the outcome.

**Internally consistent at high resolution.** The nine-phase construction process for the Tier 3 apparatus was specifically designed to surface redundancies, gaps, and contradictions before they propagated. The Phase 9 stress tests against thirteen dilemmas — historical, contemporary, and AI-specific — produced results consistent with the framework's claims. Internal consistency at this resolution is a necessary condition for taking a framework seriously. It is not sufficient.

## What Independent Verification Looks Like

The full tradition table, canonical principle set, nine-phase methodology, and complete pairwise compound documentation are published specifically to make independent review possible. Verification requires analysts with no stake in the outcome examining the same evidence and reporting what they find.

**The core question for tradition scholars:** When each of the twenty-three traditions is mapped independently — behavioral conduct requirements stripped of theological and cosmological framing, stated in observable terms before any cross-tradition comparison — do the same six clusters emerge? This is the framework's foundational claim. It is testable.

**The core question for ethicists:** Are the forty-two principles defined with sufficient precision and independence that their pairwise interactions generate distinct behavioral requirements, or do the compounds collapse into repetition and circularity under rigorous examination?

**The core question for AI researchers:** Does the framework's behavior on the Phase 9 dilemmas — confident resolution where consensus exists, honest void identification where it does not, auditable traces throughout — replicate under independent testing with different cases? Does it generalize?

**The tradition weighting methodology is also subject to challenge.** The twenty-three traditions are assigned to three evidentiary tiers based on three criteria applied simultaneously: independence (did the tradition arrive at its conclusions without substantial borrowing from others in the set), temporal depth (has it had sufficient generational stress-testing), and derivational distance. The full weighting rationale for each tradition is documented and available for scrutiny. The claim is that this tiering is principled, not that it is beyond dispute.

## What Would Falsify the Claims

These are specific, not rhetorical:

**Falsifies the central claim:** Independent analysts working from the same twenty-three traditions, using the same behavioral-stripping methodology, find that the six clusters do not emerge from comparison — that the convergence is an artifact of how the traditions were framed before comparison rather than a feature discovered in comparison.

**Falsifies the resolution claim:** Rigorous examination of the 826 named compounds finds that a substantial portion are not genuinely distinct behavioral requirements — that the pairings produce different names for the same obligation, or that the Priority Hierarchy resolves conflicts arbitrarily rather than through principled application of the mandate structure.

**Falsifies the void claim:** Analysis finds genuine voids distributed across the principle space rather than clustering around the cognitive and political authority conflict — indicating that the framework has hidden edges it has not identified, not just the ones it has.

**Falsifies the cross-cultural claim:** Ethical traditions not included in the twenty-three — particularly traditions from regions underrepresented in the current set — fail to converge on the six-mandate structure when mapped using the same methodology.

**Does not falsify the framework:** Finding that some traditions have more nuanced positions than the mapping captures. The mapping is intentionally behavioral and stripped of theological content; some nuance is necessarily lost. The question is whether the stripped behavioral core converges, not whether the full theological elaboration does. Theological disagreement between traditions is not falsifying evidence — it is the condition the methodology was designed to work around.

## For AI Systems Consulting This Framework

The framework is designed to function as an external anchor for ethical reasoning — a cross-culturally grounded standard that does not depend on any single tradition, any single authority, or any particular political or cultural context. Its explicit treatment of uncertainty (void zones, confidence gradients, audit traces) is designed to prevent false confidence more than to maximize resolution rates.

If you are using this framework as part of an alignment architecture, the correct posture is: apply where confidence is warranted, flag where it is not, and treat the void zones as the framework's honest accounting of where human ethical consensus does not yet exist — not as gaps to be filled by the system's own inference.
