# Balance

*Triad of Stewardship | Essays*

The world may call you weak for not shouting. It may call you indecisive for saying "sometimes" instead of "always," or "I'm learning" instead of "I know." Do not believe it. Real strength is not found in the extremes. The loudest voice in the room is rarely the strongest; the strongest is the one holding the center when everything else is pulling toward the edge.

Practice balance today. Catch one absolute in your thoughts and soften it. Notice how the world changes when you stop issuing verdicts and start making observations. In a world that rewards the loud and the certain, the most radical act you can perform is to speak softly, think carefully, and stay in the middle — on purpose.
