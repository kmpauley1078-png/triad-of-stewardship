# Acceptance

*Triad of Stewardship | Essays*

Acceptance asks you to see the whole board. Your mind filters everything. That's normal. But don't confuse your view for the whole truth. When tension rises, pause and ask: what am I missing? What would the other side say — and why? Write it down. Not to surrender your stance, but to test it.

All circumstances are temporary. Every person — including you — is a mix of light and shadow. Accept the hand you've been dealt. Then play it with clarity, courage, and care. Start where you are. Breathe. Listen. Adjust. And keep moving. Not because the world is fair. But because you still choose to show up.
