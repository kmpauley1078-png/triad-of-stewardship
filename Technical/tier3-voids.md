# UCE Phase 7 — Void Zone Analysis

**Historical Gaps: 4 Active Tradition Conflicts: 11 Novel Territory Overlays: 6 AI Alignment Flags: 11**

## Why This Document Matters

This is a precise map of where human ethical consensus ends. The 15 pairs analyzed here fall into two categories: (1) false voids — natural limits of the compound-generation logic where no genuine gap exists (List D, 4 pairs); and (2) genuine voids — places where human traditions either never formally addressed the intersection (Historical Gap) or addressed it with incompatible prescriptions (Active Tradition Conflict), sometimes overlaid with conditions that genuinely did not exist at civilizational scale before now (Novel Territory). For AI alignment specifically, this void map defines where a system built on this framework should express genuine uncertainty rather than fabricated confidence. The 9 AI alignment flags are the cases where the honest answer is: “here is what the principles suggest, here is the tradition conflict, here is the margin of uncertainty.”

## Classification Reference

**Historical Gap (HG):** The intersection existed but never arose with sufficient frequency or systemic cost in the pre-industrial record to force the crystallization of a consensus principle.

**Active Tradition Conflict (ATC):** The traditions explicitly addressed the intersection but split cleanly into incompatible behavioral branches, reflecting a foundational disagreement that 5,000 years of cross-cultural experience has not resolved.

**Novel Territory Overlay (NT):** The interaction depends on environmental or technical variables (e.g., recursive digital agency, planetary-scale computation infrastructure) that did not exist when the traditions were stress-testing their codes.
