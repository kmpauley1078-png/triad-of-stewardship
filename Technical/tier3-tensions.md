# UCE Phase 4 — First-Pass Sorting

861 pairs sorted into four working lists

**List A: 826 Candidate Virtues · List B: 24 Tensions · List C: 7 Redundants · List D: 4 Provisional Voids**

## **A:D Ratio Analysis**

The A:D ratio is 826:4 — approximately 207:1. This is the most significant structural finding of Phase 4. The predicted range anticipated a higher void rate; the actual distribution shows that the 42 principles are defined with sufficient precision that nearly every pairing — within and across mandates — generates a distinct, nameable conduct requirement. A framework producing mostly voids at this stage would signal that the principles are too abstract to be operationally useful. A framework producing mostly compounds signals that the principles are doing real definitional work. The 4 provisional voids are all genuinely thin intersections where both principles applied simultaneously produce no conduct requirement beyond their individual applications — they are not failures of the framework but natural limits of the pairing logic.

The 24 tensions are not voids — they are the framework’s load-bearing joints. Each one identifies a place where the Priority Hierarchy must be explicit rather than assumed. All 24 re-activate the core priority rules.

# UCE Phase 5 — Cross-Cultural Evidence Sifting (Tradition Review)

The 24 tensions (List B) submitted to the empirical evidence of the 23 traditions.

### **Methodological Filter:**
A tension is declared *Resolved by Tradition* if $\geq 18$ of the 23 weighted traditions exhibit a consistent priority assignment in their primary behavioral texts. If fewer than 18 traditions agree, or if the evidence shows a flat structural split between major historical lineages, the pair is flagged as an *Active Tradition Conflict* and transferred to Phase 7.

### **Results Summary:**
- **12 Tensions Resolved by Tradition** (Consensus $\geq 18/23$)
- **12 Tensions Unresolved by Tradition** (Transferred to Phase 7 as Active Tradition Conflicts)
